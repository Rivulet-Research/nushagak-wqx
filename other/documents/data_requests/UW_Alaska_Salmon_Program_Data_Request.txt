Subject: Water Quality Data Request — Nushagak River Watershed Baseline Assessment

---

Dear Alaska Salmon Program Team,

I am writing to request access to water quality and salmon habitat data for the Nushagak River watershed in southwestern Alaska. I am preparing a comprehensive baseline water quality assessment for the Nushagak drainage (USGS HUC6: 190303) to support a collaborative monitoring and data management initiative led by three Alaska Native communities: the Native Village of Ekwok, New Koliganek Village Council, and New Stuyahok Village Council.

**Project Context**

The three partner communities are responding to a Request for Proposal issued in 2026 for the "Nushagak River Collaborative Monitoring & Data Management Project." The initiative has two core goals:
1. Establish continuous water quality monitoring systems to track baseline conditions across the Nushagak drainage and its five major sub-basins.
2. Develop integrated data management infrastructure to archive, standardize, and provide access to monitoring data in service of salmon conservation and subsistence management.

To design an effective monitoring strategy, we are compiling all available existing water quality and salmon habitat data for the watershed. This baseline assessment will identify current data gaps and inform the design of the new collaborative monitoring network.

**Data Request**

I would like to inquire whether the Alaska Salmon Program maintains water quality, stream habitat, or salmon population data for any of the following areas:

**Primary Target Watersheds:**
- Nushagak River mainstem and upper tributaries (HUC8: 19030301)
- Mulchatna River drainage (HUC8: 19030302)
- Nuyakuk River drainage (HUC8: 19030303)
- Wood River drainage (HUC8: 19030304)
- Snake River / Lower Nushagak (HUC8: 19030305)

**Data Types of Interest:**
- Stream temperature (continuous monitoring or spot measurements)
- Water chemistry (pH, dissolved oxygen, nutrients, trace metals)
- Salmon presence/absence, abundance, run timing, or age composition
- Stream habitat metrics (substrate, flow, riparian condition)
- Fish migration or behavior data
- Any long-term datasets documenting changes over time

The baseline water quality assessment will be compiled into a public-facing Quarto book (R-based HTML document) that will be distributed to the three partner communities and made available to other researchers and managers interested in the Nushagak watershed. All data sources will be clearly cited and acknowledged.
